About & Citation

What this is

This site is the interactive digital companion to the manuscript “Generative AI in Medical Education and Scholarship: A Practical Framework and Prompt Toolkit from Pathology” by Omar Z. Baba, MD (Henry Ford Hospital), Tanmay Lele, PhD (Texas A&M University), and Srikar Chamala, PhD (Children's Hospital Los Angeles / Keck School of Medicine, USC). It renders the paper's four-pillar framework as guided, scenario-based walkthroughs and an openly available prompt library.

The library comprises 102 prompts: 88 developed for and deployed at a hands-on session at the 2026 Association for Pathology Informatics Bootcamp (Pathology Informatics Summit), covering learning; teaching, curriculum, and assessment; and workflow and operations — extended here with 14 scholarship prompts drawn directly from the manuscript, so the companion covers all four pillars. Each entry pairs a prompt with its intent, expected output, common failure modes, and an explicit human-verification step.

What this is not

Not a guide to building diagnostic AI, computational pipelines, or laboratory methods. The framework concerns educational and scholarly uses of readily available, general-purpose tools. No protected health information or real patient data appears anywhere; every clinical example is hypothetical, de-identified, and generic. The framework is expert-derived rather than a validation study, and none of its recommendations have been tested against learning outcomes — it asserts plausibility and supplies guardrails.

Model outputs are non-deterministic and version-dependent. The tools named, tier distinctions drawn, and exact prompts offered will change as models and vendor policies evolve. The durable content is the task-to-tool-to-verification logic, not any particular product or setting.

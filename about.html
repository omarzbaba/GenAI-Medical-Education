About & Citation

What this is

This site is the supplementary digital companion to the manuscript “Generative AI in Medical Education and Scholarship: A Practical Framework and Prompt Toolkit from Pathology.” It is meant to be read hand in hand with the paper: the pillars, prompts, figures, tables, worked example, and guardrails presented here are the manuscript's own, rendered as guided walkthroughs and a browsable library. Wherever a page draws on the paper, a Manuscript · … tag points to the exact section, table, or figure.

The library

132 prompts, built in three layers. The base is 88 prompts developed for and deployed at a hands-on session at the 2026 Association for Pathology Informatics Bootcamp (Pathology Informatics Summit), covering learning; teaching, curriculum, and assessment; and workflow and operations. That was extended with 15 scholarship prompts — 14 drawn directly from the manuscript's Section 5 and Tables 4–5, plus one (title/abstract screening) grounded in a task the manuscript discusses but does not itself template — so the companion covers all four pillars, mirroring manuscript Table 2. A third layer of 29 prompts, spanning all four pillars, was added afterward to broaden practical day-to-day coverage beyond what either the workshop or the manuscript specifically addresses; these are original material, not manuscript- or workshop-derived, and are marked as such in each entry's front matter. Every entry, regardless of origin, pairs a prompt with its intent, expected output, common failure modes, and an explicit human-verification step.

What this is not

Not a guide to building diagnostic AI, computational pipelines, or laboratory methods. The framework concerns educational and scholarly uses of readily available, general-purpose tools. No protected health information or real patient data appears anywhere; every clinical example is hypothetical, de-identified, and generic. The framework is expert-derived rather than a validation study, and none of its recommendations have been tested against learning outcomes — it asserts plausibility and supplies guardrails.

Model outputs are non-deterministic and version-dependent. The tools named, tier distinctions drawn, and exact prompts offered will change as models and vendor policies evolve. The durable content is the task-to-tool-to-verification logic, not any particular product or setting.

A Zenodo archive DOI for the prompt library will be added at manuscript acceptance.
